Welcome

Feb 2016 · 2016-02-27

Hello! Welcome to Elle and Adam's blog. As soon as we get going we'll be posting here to keep all our friends and family updated as to where we are and what we're up to.

Our plan is to spend 10 months travelling the world. We'll start in Santiago, Chile and head north right up to Canada before flying to Iceland and onto mainland Europe. Our last stop will be Dubai for Christmas and then home on 27 December.

We will post here when we can but it will usually be a little bit behind where we actually are. Check out our progress on route on the home page.
